Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

StudentKit (“we”, “our”, or “the site”) provides free student tools, including attendance, CGPA, study planning, budgeting, and resume tools. This policy explains what information may be handled when you use the site.

Information you enter

The calculators and tools on StudentKit run in your browser. The information you enter into these tools is used to generate results on your device; the site’s tool code does not send those entries to a StudentKit server or store them in an account.

Cookies and advertising

We may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, and personalize ads where permitted. These technologies may collect information such as your browser, device, approximate location, and interactions with ads.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services in Google’s partner-site policy. You can manage ad personalization through Google My Ad Center and manage cookies in your browser settings. Depending on your location, additional consent choices may be presented.

Third-party services

Advertising and other third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies. StudentKit does not control how third parties process information collected through their services.

Children’s privacy

StudentKit is a general student resource and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us through the repository link below so we can review the concern.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the StudentKit GitHub repository.
